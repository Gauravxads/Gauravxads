<h2 align="center">🚀 About Me</h2>

**Gaurav** here, a digital marketer from Mumbai who builds his own apps.

By day I run SEO, Meta ads, social media and content. By night I ship Android and web apps for myself, my friends and small businesses around me.

Currently building with **Next.js, Supabase, Firebase, Capacitor and Kotlin**.

My goal is simple: spot a real problem, build the smallest app that fixes it, and get it into people's hands.
